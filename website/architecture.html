CRC cards

Responsibilities and collaborators of the main classes.

ClassResponsibilitiesCollaborators
GameScreenCreates one game; reads the keys and mouse; calls the other parts once per frame in a fixed order; draws the map and playerGameSession, Player, Inventory, EventSpot, MapManager, Hud
GameSessionKnows the state (ready, playing, paused, won, lost); starts and pauses the timer; decides the result; counts eventsGameTimer, WaveStatus
GameTimerCounts the 5 minutes down only while running; formats the time for the HUD–
PlayerMoves in small steps, stops at walls and the map edge; applies and ends speed boosts; finds a free start spotCollisionChecker
Inventory / ItemHolds up to nine items; selects one; uses it where the player clicked; enforces uses and cooldowns; counts items usedGooseLocator, Feedable, SpeedBoostTarget
EventSpotTriggers its hidden event once when the player walks into range, then runs the event’s effectIHiddenEvent
MapManager / CollisionManagerLoads and draws the Tiled campus map; reads the collision rectangles; answers “is this space blocked?”libGDX TmxMapLoader
HudDraws the time, goals, counters, inventory bar, messages and the start, pause and result screens, always with text as well as colourGameSession, Inventory, Player, WaveStatus
GooseManagerSends the waves; moves and separates the geese; finds geese for the items; blocks the player; startles the player when a snatch lands; reports when every wave is fedGoose, Wave, GooseListener, SpeedBoostTarget
GooseWalks to the player, flapping over obstacles; warns, then snatches; fills its hunger bar and flies away when fullGooseType, CollisionChecker
