Goose Patrol

Waves of hungry geese are coming across the University of York campus. Feed them until they fly away, and clear every wave before the 5-minute timer runs out. No geese are harmed.

The game

  • A 5-minute countdown timer that is always on screen, with a pause option
  • Waves of geese to feed and send on their way
  • A map based on the University of York campus
  • Two items in your inventory at the start
  • Two hidden events waiting to be found
  • Counters for the items you use and the events you trigger

Built in Java 17 with LibGDX.

Play the game

Download GoosePatrol-1.0.0.jar (about 17 MB). It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux with Java 17 or newer. Double-click it, or run java -jar GoosePatrol-1.0.0.jar

How to play: three waves of hungry geese come to Campus East. Click a goose with the Bag of Oats (key 1), or throw Bread (key 2) near a group. A full goose flies away happily. Feed every goose before the 5 minutes run out. A goose that flashes red and honks wants your snack, so step away or feed it. Walk into the ? boxes to see what they do.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to walk, 1–9 to choose an item, left click to use it, P or Esc to pause, M for sound on or off, R to play again after a game.
