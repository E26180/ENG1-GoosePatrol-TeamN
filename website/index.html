Goose Patrol

Waves of hungry geese are coming across the University of York campus. Feed them until they fly away, and clear every wave before the 5-minute timer runs out. No geese are harmed.

The game

  • A 5-minute countdown timer that is always on screen, with a pause option
  • Waves of geese to feed and send on their way
  • A map based on the University of York campus
  • Two items in your inventory at the start
  • Two hidden events waiting to be found
  • Counters for the items you use and the events you trigger

Built in Java 17 with LibGDX.
